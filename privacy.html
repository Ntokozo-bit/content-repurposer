PRIVACY POLICY

Your content should remain yours.

Last updated: July 10, 2026

This policy explains the current browser-based version of RepurposerHub. It will be updated if the way the service processes data changes.

Draft content

The text you enter into the main RepurposerHub workspace is processed locally in your browser. The current writing engine does not send that draft text to a RepurposerHub application server or an external artificial-intelligence provider.

You should still avoid entering confidential, sensitive or legally protected information into any online tool unless you are comfortable doing so.

Browser storage

RepurposerHub uses local browser storage to remember your draft, generation settings, theme and cookie preference. This information remains on the device and browser profile you used.

You can remove it by clearing this site's stored data in your browser. Clearing browser data may also remove your saved draft and preferences.

Optional analytics and advertising

If you choose “Accept” in the privacy banner, RepurposerHub may load Google Analytics and Google AdSense. These services may process information such as browser and device details, approximate location derived from an IP address, page visits, interactions, cookies or similar identifiers.

These optional scripts are not intentionally loaded by the main workspace before consent is accepted. Choosing “Essential only” leaves the local workspace available without optional analytics or advertising scripts.

Google processes information under its own policies. You can learn more through Google's Privacy Policy and advertising technologies information.

Your choices

  • Choose “Essential only” when the privacy banner appears.
  • Open “Cookie settings” in the website footer to change your preference.
  • Clear this site's local storage through your browser settings.
  • Use browser controls or extensions to restrict cookies and tracking technologies.
  • Contact us with a privacy question or request.

External links and services

RepurposerHub may link to third-party websites. Their privacy practices are controlled by those third parties, not by RepurposerHub. Review their policies before providing information to them.

Children

RepurposerHub is a general-purpose writing tool and is not intentionally directed at children. Do not submit personal information about a child through the service.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the product changes. The date at the top of the page will be revised when material changes are published.
