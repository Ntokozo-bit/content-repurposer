ABOUT THE PRODUCT

Make every good idea go further.

RepurposerHub exists to reduce the repetitive work between having an idea and publishing it in the right format.

What RepurposerHub does

RepurposerHub turns a source draft, transcript, announcement or rough note into editable posts for X, LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp.

Instead of copying the same paragraph everywhere, the tool adapts the hook, structure, length and call to action for each platform.

Why it was built

Creators and small businesses often have useful ideas but limited time. Rewriting the same message for several platforms can turn publishing into a chore.

Our goal is to provide a fast first draft that users can refine with their own knowledge, personality and judgement.

Privacy-first by design

The current writing engine runs in the browser. Draft text is processed on the user's device rather than being uploaded to a RepurposerHub application server.

The browser can save the draft and settings locally so work is not lost between visits.
