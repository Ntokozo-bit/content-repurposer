FAQ

Good to know

Is RepurposerHub really free?

Yes. The core browser-based tool is free to use without creating an account.

Is my content sent to a server?

No. This version processes the text in your browser. Your draft can be saved in local browser storage so you can continue later, but it is not uploaded to our servers.

Does it use artificial intelligence?

The free version uses a local rule-based writing engine. It restructures and adapts your content without sending it to an external AI provider.

Can I edit the generated posts?

Yes. Every result is editable. Treat the output as a strong first draft and add your own voice before publishing.
